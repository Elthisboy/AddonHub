# AddonHub privacy policy

AddonHub is an **unofficial** app to download and install community content for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Mojang, Microsoft, or Overwolf / CurseForge.

## In short

- No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
- We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
- What parents configure stays on the device.

## What the app stores on the device

Only on the device itself (it is never sent to any server):

- **Parental settings:** the chosen age profile, a 4-digit PIN (stored only as an irreversible hash, never
  the PIN itself), blocked words and the items an adult approved.
- **Adult mode:** if an adult turns it on, they are asked for their date of birth only to check they are
  18 or older. **The date is not stored or sent anywhere**; the app only remembers that the check was done.
- **Favorites and history:** only content ID numbers and the install date.
- **Temporary downloads:** files are kept in the app's temporary storage until they are imported into
  Minecraft, and are deleted when the next download starts.

The app does **not** allow cloud backups of this data.

## What leaves the device

To show the catalog and download content, the app connects to:

1. **Our intermediary server** (hosted on Cloudflare), which queries CurseForge on the app's behalf. What
   the person types in the search box, the category being browsed and the items requested pass through it.
   **We do not store** this data or link it to a person. Like any internet service, Cloudflare may process
   technical data such as the IP address so the service works, under its own policy.
2. **CurseForge**, whose CDN delivers the files. Its handling of data is governed by its own policy.

We do not share or sell any information to third parties.

## Children

The app is designed for children aged 6 to 15 and **does not collect personal data from them**. An adult
chooses the age profile and creates the PIN at first launch. The content filter hides content that is not
appropriate for the chosen age and always blocks sexual content; it is not perfect, and parents can block
extra words.

## Notifications

The app shows local notifications about download progress. It does not use remote notifications.

## Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our servers.
To erase what is stored on the device: Android Settings → Apps → AddonHub → Storage → Clear data (this also
resets the parental settings and the PIN).

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/Elthisboy/AddonHub/issues).
