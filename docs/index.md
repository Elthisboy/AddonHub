# AddonHub

**A free, kid-friendly Android app to install Minecraft Bedrock content from CurseForge in a few taps.**

> AddonHub is an **unofficial** app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Mojang,
> Microsoft, or Overwolf / CurseForge. "Minecraft" is a trademark of Mojang Synergies AB.

## What it does

Kids who play Minecraft Bedrock on Android often struggle to install add-ons and maps: find a trustworthy
site, download a file, locate it, open it with Minecraft. AddonHub turns that into a couple of taps:

1. **Browse** add-ons, maps, texture packs and skins, with search and categories.
2. **Preview** the content: screenshots, description, author and what else it needs.
3. **Install**: the app downloads the file and hands it to Minecraft, which imports it automatically.

Downloads keep going in the background, and are resumed if the connection drops.

## Respect for creators

- All content comes from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock). AddonHub **does not
  host or re-upload any files**: every install is an official download from CurseForge's CDN.
- Only content whose author **allows distribution** is shown or installed.
- The author's name is shown on every item, the dependencies the author declares are respected, and adults
  can open each item's CurseForge page from inside the app (behind the parent PIN).
- No ads and no paywall: nobody profits from creators' work. Creators can ask for their content to be
  removed at any time, and it will be removed immediately.

## Made for kids, with parental controls

- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no personal data collected.
- A parent sets the child's age profile and a 4-digit PIN on first launch.
- A content filter hides content that is not appropriate for the chosen age, and always blocks sexual
  content. Parents can block extra words and approve specific items.
- Opening links outside the app requires the parent's PIN.
